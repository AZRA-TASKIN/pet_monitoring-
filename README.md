**PetSense AI – Intelligent Pet Health and Routine Monitoring System**

PetSense AI is an AI-assisted pet care platform designed to help pet owners monitor their pets’ daily activities, health indicators, and care routines through an interactive dashboard. It includes pet profile management, feeding schedules, walking reminders, medication tracking, grooming tasks, and routine progress monitoring.

The system demonstrates the potential of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) through activity classification, health anomaly detection, behaviour analysis, sleep monitoring, and predictive scheduling. These technologies can analyse pet data to identify unusual patterns and generate personalised care recommendations.

PetSense AI can also integrate Internet of Things (IoT) devices, such as wearable sensors, temperature monitors, motion sensors, and cameras, to collect real-time information. The current prototype uses simulated health readings and AI insights, while integrating trained and validated ML models is planned for future development.

The main objective of PetSense AI is to make pet care more organised, convenient, and data-driven. It aims to help owners maintain consistent care routines and recognise changes that may require attention. The platform is intended to support pet owners and complement, not replace, professional veterinary advice.
